# Maze-Game
🔒 Dungeon Escape – A 2D Maze Adventure
Welcome to Dungeon Escape — a thrilling retro-inspired 2D maze game that drops you into the heart of a dark and dangerous dungeon. The walls whisper secrets. The air is thick with tension. Somewhere in the shadows, something watches you.

Your mission? Simple on paper. Find the key. Avoid the traps. Reach the exit. But nothing is ever that easy in the Dungeon...

🧩 Game Features
Challenging Mazes: Each level is a handcrafted labyrinth filled with twists, turns, and tricks.
Deadly Hazards: One wrong step could trigger hidden spikes or crushing traps. Stay sharp!
Retro Pixel Art Style: Nostalgic visuals designed with love for fans of old-school games.
Atmospheric Soundtrack: Eerie and immersive music that reacts to your gameplay.
Progressive Difficulty: Levels get harder as you delve deeper. Precision and timing are key.
Speedrun Friendly: Can you master each level and beat your fastest escape time?
🎮 Controls
Use Arrow keys or WASD to move — E to get the key and open the door, F to kill enemies. Every move counts — there’s no room for hesitation in the Dungeon.

💡 Will you escape… or become another lost soul trapped forever?
